# Interview Notes Template

Copy this once per interview and keep the copy **private** (your device or a private drive). Do not commit filled-in copies to this repo. Only an anonymised summary goes to `docs/Research/interview-findings.md`.

## Rules

- Write what they **said**, in their words. Put your own interpretations under "My interpretation" only.
- Quotes go in the language they were said in, with English beside them.
- No names. Students are S1, S2, …; staff are "the HOD" or "department staff".
- Same day: pull quotes from the audio into this sheet, then delete the audio.
