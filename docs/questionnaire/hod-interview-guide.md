# HOD Interview Guide

One conversation, 10–15 minutes. Ask in this order: the must-ask questions block later decisions; the if-time questions can also be answered by the IT desk or the college website. Use the [notes template](interview-notes-template.md).

## Rules for you

- Ask about what **actually happened**, not what should happen.
- Don't defend or pitch the idea; you're here to learn how things work today.
- Don't record their name anywhere. In notes and the repo they are "the HOD", or "department staff" for anything sensitive.

## Consent (30 seconds)

> "Thank you for making time. This is for my course project. I'd like to understand how repairs get done today, so the tool I build fits how you work rather than adding work. May I record audio so I don't misquote you? I'll delete it after writing my notes. Anything I quote in the project will be anonymous."

- Recording not OK → take notes by hand, and before leaving read your key quotes back: "Did I get this right?"

## Must-ask

1. **The repair chain.** "When a student's written application reaches you, what happens next?"
   - Who do you call? Is the carpenter in-house or from outside?
   - Does anyone else need to approve it — the estate office, spending approval?
   - "Why does it need to be a written application?"
2. **Proof.** "What do you need to see to trust that a problem is real?"
   - Is a photo enough, or do you need the time, the room and the seat too?
   - "Have you had fake or exaggerated complaints? What happened?"
3. **Hearing about reports.** "How would you want to find out about a new problem?"
   - Each one as it comes in, a daily list, on WhatsApp, by email — what fits your day?
4. **Status updates.** "If students could see the status — 'seen', 'carpenter called', 'fixed' — would you or someone you name keep that updated?"
   - Who would it be? How often could they realistically do it?
5. **Naming places.** "When you tell the carpenter where a problem is, how do you describe it?"
   - Room numbers, block names, seat rows? Is there a list or register of rooms?

## If time

6. "Is college email on Google, Microsoft, or something else?"
7. "Does the college already have a grievance or complaints portal? Does it cover repairs and facilities?"

## Close

8. "Could you walk me through the **last** repair you arranged, from when you heard about it to when it was fixed?"
   - How did you hear? Who did what? How long did it take? How did the student find out?

> "Thank you. Could I come back to you with a short list of what I learned, to check I understood?"
